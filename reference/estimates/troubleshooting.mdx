This page covers every rule that blocks, disables, warns, or requires permission when you create, edit, and move estimates — what you see, why it happens, and how to resolve it.

---

### Where did the Calculator go?

- **Symptom:** You can't find the Calculator, or an old Calculator link or bookmark opens an estimate's proposal or the **Create Estimate** dialog instead.
- **Cause:** The step-by-step Calculator has been replaced. You now build a price-book estimate directly on its proposal page. Old links still work: a link to a specific estimate opens that estimate's proposal, and any other Calculator link opens **Create Estimate**.
- **Resolution:** Start new estimates with **New Estimate** → **From Pricebook**, and edit existing ones from their proposal. See [Build an estimate](/guides/estimates/create).

### Why don't I see the "From Pricebook" option?

- **Symptom:** In the **Create Estimate** dialog, only **From Scratch** is offered.
- **Cause:** Two things can put price-book estimates out of reach:
  - **Your role.** Only certain roles can build estimates from the price book.
  - **Your plan.** Your plan may not include the price book — for example, on a free trial. Existing price-book estimates then show **Editing this proposal isn't included in your plan**: you can still view and send them, but not change them.
- **Resolution:** If it's your role, ask an admin for a role that can build price-book estimates. If it's your plan, build estimates with the [From Scratch](/guides/estimates/create-from-scratch) option, or ask an admin about upgrading your plan to add the price book.

### Why am I told I've hit the limit of open estimates?

- **Symptom:** When you create an estimate, the **Create Estimate** dialog shows a red message: "You have reached the maximum of N open estimates for this branch."
- **Cause:** When you create a *new* estimate, your open estimates for the branch (those in **In Progress** or **Released** status) are counted against your open-estimates limit. That limit is your branch's **Default Max Open Estimates**, unless an Admin has set a personal limit on your profile, which takes precedence. Once you reach that limit, you can't start another new estimate until you clear one.
- **Resolution:** Move one of your existing **In Progress** / **Released** estimates to **Sold** or **Lost**, then start the new estimate. The limit only applies to creating new estimates — editing an existing one is never affected.

### Why can't I edit this estimate? (read-only banners)

- **Symptom:** The estimate's proposal opens with no editing tools — no **Add work area**, **Edit Item**, or **Delete Item** — and a banner at the top explains why.
- **Cause:** It depends on the banner:
  - **This proposal is Sold, so it can't be edited** (or Released, Lost, and so on) — only **In Progress** estimates can be edited. Any other status (**Sold**, **Released**, **Lost**, **Secondary Estimate**) is read-only.
  - **This proposal is signed, so it can't be edited** — a signed proposal is never editable.
  - **View only** — your role can view the proposal but not change it, or the estimate belongs to another salesperson (a **Sales Member** can edit only their own estimates).
  - **Editing this proposal isn't included in your plan** — see the card above.
- **Resolution:** Change the estimate's status back to **In Progress** from the status badge at the top of the proposal, or from the estimates list. This requires the right role. If the proposal is signed, this starts a change order (see below). Until you send the proposal again, the customer sees "This proposal is currently being updated."

### Why does my change fail with "Only In Progress estimates can be edited"?

- **Symptom:** An edit is rejected with "Only In Progress estimates can be edited."
- **Cause:** The estimate's status is no longer **In Progress** — for example, someone changed it while you had the proposal open. Edits are only allowed on **In Progress** estimates, and this is checked again each time a change is saved, even if the page still looked editable.
- **Resolution:** Reload the proposal. If you still need to make the change, set the status back to **In Progress** first.

---

### Why did my edit disappear with "This estimate changed"?

- **Symptom:** After an edit, a message reads **This estimate changed** — "Someone else saved a change. We reloaded the latest version — make your edit again."
- **Cause:** Changes on the proposal save automatically. Someone else saved a change to the same estimate after you opened it, so your edit wasn't applied on top of theirs.
- **Resolution:** Check the reloaded estimate, then make your edit again.

### Why can't I save changes to a work area? ("isn't in this estimate's price book")

- **Symptom:** A change fails with "“Area name” isn't in this estimate's price book, so changes can't be saved. Use Update to current pricing first."
- **Cause:** Each estimate keeps a copy of the price book from when it was created. The work area isn't in that copy — for example, it was added to your price book later.
- **Resolution:** A manager can choose **Update to current pricing** in the proposal's **⋮** menu to bring the estimate up to date, then try again. This also updates the estimate's other prices and settings to your current ones.

### Why can't I use "Update to current pricing"?

- **Symptom:** The **⋮** menu on the proposal has no **Update to current pricing** option.
- **Cause:** It's available only to managers (Admin, Ops Manager, Sales Admin), only on estimates built from the price book, and only while the estimate can still be edited (**In Progress** and not signed).
- **Resolution:** Ask a manager to update the pricing, or move the estimate back to **In Progress** first.

### Why won't my discount go higher? ("Your role can give up to N%")

- **Symptom:** You enter a discount and it drops back to a lower number, with the note "Your role can give up to N%."
- **Cause:** Your branch's **Max Discount** caps the discount you can give. Managers can give up to 100%. A higher discount a manager already set stays in place unless you change it.
- **Resolution:** Use a discount within your limit, or ask a manager to set a higher one.

### Why don't I see the "Contractor prices" switch?

- **Symptom:** The **Contractor prices** switch next to the discount is missing on the web app.
- **Cause:** On the web app, the switch is hidden on narrow, phone-sized screens.
- **Resolution:** Widen the browser window or use a larger screen. The switch is also available in the mobile app.

### Why can't I send or present the proposal? ("Fill in the inspection report first")

- **Symptom:** **Send Proposal** and **Present to Client** are disabled with the note **Fill in the inspection report first**, and a banner reads **Fill in the inspection report before sending**. **Released** and **Sold** are greyed out in the status menu with the same note, and changing the status from the estimates list fails with "Fill in the inspection report first."
- **Cause:** If inspection reports are turned on for your workspace, every estimate built from the price book needs a completed inspection report before its proposal can be sent or presented, and before it can be moved to **Released** or **Sold**. Estimates created **From Scratch** never need one.
- **Resolution:** Click **Fill in inspection report →** in the banner (or choose **Inspection Report** in the **⋮** menu), answer the required questions, and click **Save Report**. The menu then shows the report as **Done**.

### Why can't I save the inspection report? ("Report incomplete")

- **Symptom:** Clicking **Save Report** shows **Report incomplete** — "Answer the required questions before saving."
- **Cause:** The report requires what needs to happen before the crews arrive, customer demeanor, customer concerns, the home, attic, and crawlspace square footage (each a whole number greater than zero), why the square footages differ, the number of stories, the parking distance, any concerning pets, special instructions, and crew notes. Crew notes also have a length limit ("Crew notes must be 5000 characters or fewer.").
- **Resolution:** Fill in every required question, keep crew notes within the limit, and save again.

### Why can't I edit the inspection report?

- **Symptom:** The inspection report opens read-only with no **Edit Report** button, or shows "No inspection report has been filled in yet."
- **Cause:** The report can be changed only while the estimate is editable: **In Progress**, not signed, and by someone allowed to edit the estimate. In any other status, or once the proposal is signed, the report is read-only.
- **Resolution:** Move the estimate back to **In Progress** if you need to change the report.

### Permissions: who can create / edit / delete an estimate?

- **Symptom:** An action is denied, or a button is hidden, when you try to create, edit, or delete an estimate.
- **Cause:** Creating and editing estimates are available to Admin, Ops Manager, Sales Admin, Sales Member, and Client Coordinator. Crew Leader can edit crew notes only, not the estimate itself. Deleting is reserved for Admin, Ops Manager, and Sales Admin. The delete button is hidden for roles that can't delete.
- **Resolution:** Use a role with the permission you need. Sales Member and Client Coordinator can create and edit but not delete. Ops Manager can manage estimates but with status restrictions (see the next card).

### Why can't I change the status of a Sold estimate?

- **Symptom:** The status dropdown won't let you move a **Sold** estimate to another status, or the server rejects with "Sold estimates cannot be modified without the required role."
- **Cause:** Moving an estimate out of **Sold** requires management-level permissions on estimates. This is enforced both in the dropdown and when the change is saved.
- **Resolution:** Use a role with management-level estimate permissions (for example, Admin or Sales Admin) to change a Sold estimate's status.

---

### Why can't I set certain statuses (Released / Sold / Lost / Secondary)? (Ops Manager)

- **Symptom:** As an Ops Manager you can edit estimates, but the status dropdown won't let you set **Released**, **Sold**, **Lost**, **Secondary Estimate**, or **Deleted**.
- **Cause:** Ops Manager can manage estimates but can only set their status to **In Progress**. The other status transitions are reserved for Sales roles.
- **Resolution:** A Sales role — Sales Member, Sales Admin, or Client Coordinator — must perform sale, loss, release, and secondary transitions.

### Why can't I mark this estimate Sold? ("project already has a sold estimate" or "already has an active job")

- **Symptom:** The **Sold** option is greyed out in the status menu, or the change is rejected with a message like "This project already has a sold estimate ("…"). Only one estimate per project can be sold — change that estimate's status first." If the project's winning estimate was reopened for a change order but its job is still running, the message instead reads "This project already has an active job for "…". Only one estimate per project can have an active job — re-sell that estimate, or cancel its job first."
- **Cause:** A project can have several estimates, but only one can be the winner. Another estimate in the same project is already Sold — or still has an active (not closed or cancelled) job, which happens when the winner was reopened for a change order — so this one can't also be sold. The same block applies when a customer tries to sign this estimate's proposal, and it closes the proposal-send path for the project's other estimates (see the next card).
- **Resolution:** Decide which estimate is the winner. To make this one the winner, move the currently sold estimate in the project out of **Sold** (which needs management-level permissions); if that estimate already has a job, cancel the job too. Then mark this one **Sold**. Re-selling the current winner (for example, for a change order) is never blocked.

### Why are "Send Proposal" and "Present to Client" disabled? ("another estimate is already sold or has an active job")

- **Symptom:** On the proposal preview, the **Send Proposal** button (or **Resend Proposal**, if it was sent before) and **Present to Client** are disabled, and hovering **Send** shows "Another estimate in this project is already sold or has an active job, so this proposal can't be sent or presented."
- **Cause:** Once one estimate in a project is the winner — either Sold, or holding an active (not closed or cancelled) job — that project's proposal cycle is closed. The other estimates in the project can no longer send *or* present their proposals; presenting is held back too because it ends in the customer's signature, which is refused for the same reason. The block is applied up front rather than failing after the fact.
- **Resolution:** If this estimate should be the winner instead, move the current winner out of **Sold** (which needs management-level permissions) and cancel its job if it already has one; **Send** and **Present** then become available again. Re-selling or re-sending the current winner is never blocked.

### Why am I asked to start a change order when I change a signed estimate's status?

- **Symptom:** Changing a signed estimate's status to **In Progress** opens **Start a change order?**, explaining the customer's signature will be removed; **Start change order** stays disabled until you enter **Change order notes**.
- **Cause:** The proposal has been signed, and moving the estimate back to **In Progress** requires removing that signature first. Instead of applying the change immediately, the app asks you to start a change order. The signed proposal is kept as a read-only earlier version.
- **Resolution:** Enter change order notes (visible to your client) and click **Start change order**. This clears the signature, payment method, and financing term together with the status change, and shows a "Signature removed" confirmation. You need management-level permissions — without them you'll see "You do not have permission to remove a customer signature."

### Why can't I delete this estimate? ("signed, cannot be deleted")

- **Symptom:** The delete button is present, but its tooltip reads "This estimate was signed and cannot be deleted," or the server rejects with "Cannot delete an estimate with a signed proposal." It can also reject with "This estimate has an active job. Cancel the job before deleting the estimate."
- **Cause:** An estimate whose proposal has been signed can't be deleted. Separately, a **Sold** estimate can't be deleted either ("Sold estimates cannot be deleted."). And an estimate that still has an active (not closed or cancelled) job can't be deleted — deleting it would strand that job and keep every other estimate in the project from selling.
- **Resolution:** For a signed estimate, remove the signature first (which requires management-level permissions). A **Sold** estimate can't be deleted this way — change its status first if appropriate. If the estimate has an active job, cancel the job before deleting the estimate.

### Why can't I edit crew notes on a deleted estimate?

- **Symptom:** Editing crew notes fails with "This estimate has been deleted."
- **Cause:** Crew notes can be edited even on estimates that are otherwise locked (such as **Sold**), but not on a **Deleted** estimate.
- **Resolution:** Crew notes can be edited on any non-deleted estimate by a role with crew-note permissions. A deleted estimate must be restored first.

---

### Why can't I change the "Sold Date" (effective sold date)?

- **Symptom:** The sold-date picker shows as plain read-only text with no calendar button, or saving a date fails.
- **Cause:** The sold date can be changed only by Admin, Ops Manager, or Sales Admin; for everyone else it appears as read-only text. It only appears at all when the estimate is **Sold** and already has a sold date. Future dates can't be selected, and the date can only be set on **Sold** estimates ("Effective sold date can only be set on Sold estimates.").
- **Resolution:** As an Admin, Ops Manager, or Sales Admin, pick a date that isn't in the future on a **Sold** estimate.

---

### Why can't I set "first sold date" or "status changed at" directly?

- **Symptom:** These sidebar fields are read-only and can't be changed.
- **Cause:** Both are maintained automatically by the system. The first sold date is set the first time an estimate becomes **Sold** and never changes afterward; the status-changed date is stamped automatically whenever the status changes.
- **Resolution:** These can't be edited by anyone. To adjust sales attribution, use the **Sold Date** field instead (see the previous card).

---

### Why did my status change fail with a generic error toast?

- **Symptom:** A toast reading "Unable to update status" with a server message, or "A network error occurred. Please check your connection and try again."
- **Cause:** The status change failed — either it was rejected (for example, one of the Sold-role or read-only rules above) or a network error occurred.
- **Resolution:** Read the toast message — it relays the specific reason (such as the Sold-role or read-only messages above) — and address that cause. For network errors, simply retry.

---

### Server-side "Invalid ID" / "Estimate not found" errors

- **Symptom:** "Invalid estimate ID." or "Estimate not found." returned from an estimate operation.
- **Cause:** The estimate reference was invalid ("Invalid estimate ID."), or the estimate or its branch could no longer be found ("Estimate not found." / "Estimate branch could not be resolved.").
- **Resolution:** This is usually a transient issue or a stale link — reload the estimates list and retry with a valid estimate.
