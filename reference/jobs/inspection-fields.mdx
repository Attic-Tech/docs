This reference explains the inspection report that a salesperson completes for an estimate: what it records, which fields are required, who can create or change it, and the rules that govern when it can be saved or edited.

The user-facing roles referenced below are **Admin**, **Ops Manager**, **Sales Admin**, **Sales Member**, **Client Coordinator**, **Crew Leader**, and **Crew Member**. For the full breakdown of what each role can do, see [/reference/platform/permissions-roles-matrix](/reference/platform/permissions-roles-matrix).

## What the inspection report is

The inspection report is a structured record the salesperson completes for an estimate. It captures what they observed during the in-person walkthrough so the install crew can work confidently, avoid mistakes, and deliver a consistent customer experience.

There is exactly **one inspection report per estimate**. The report is tied to a single estimate and the link is one-to-one — there is never more than one report for the same estimate.

The report is organized into themed sections:

- Scheduling and Job Setup
- Customer Profile
- Property Details
- Walkthrough Notes
- Roof Information
- HVAC Information
- Crew Notes

## When the inspection report is available

The inspection report only exists when your workspace has the inspection-report feature turned on, and only on estimates built from your price book. Estimates built from scratch never have one.

When the feature is on:

- **You open the report from the estimate's proposal.** On the web, open the proposal's **⋮** (**More actions**) menu and choose **Inspection Report**. In the mobile app, it's in the ⋮ menu at the top of the proposal. The menu item shows a **Done** or **Not started** badge; any report that isn't complete counts as **Not started**.
- **The report is required.** Until it's complete, the proposal can't be sent or presented to the client, and the estimate can't be moved to **Released** or **Sold**. The proposal shows a **Fill in the inspection report before sending** banner, with a **Fill in inspection report** link that opens the report.

For how this fits into building an estimate, see [Build an estimate](/guides/estimates/create).

## Who can create or change a report

<Note>
  Inspection-report access follows your workspace's role permissions. See [/reference/platform/permissions-roles-matrix](/reference/platform/permissions-roles-matrix) for the authoritative breakdown, and [/reference/platform/org-branch-scoping](/reference/platform/org-branch-scoping) for how visibility is scoped to your organization.
</Note>

- **Create and edit:** Admin, Ops Manager, Sales Admin, Sales Member, and Client Coordinator.
- **Your own estimates only:** on another salesperson's estimate, the report is read-only unless your role can see every client.
- **View:** scoped to the user's own organization; most staff roles can read a report, and some roles are read-only.
- **Delete:** inspection reports are not deletable through the product; they are removed only by Menaia platform staff.

## What the report records

The lists below note which fields are **required** before the report can be saved. A few fields are required by the form even though the saved record itself would accept them empty — those are called out as "required in the form."

### Scheduling and Job Setup

- Whether the customer is flexible on scheduling — **required**.
- Whether anything needs to happen before the crews arrive (for example, sprinklers, abatement, or roof work) — **required** free text.

The estimated days of work is no longer part of the report. It's set under **Job setup → Days of work** on the estimate's proposal, where it drives travel, load and unload time, and gas.

### Customer Profile

- A description of the customer's demeanor (for example, calm, detail-oriented, emotional) — **required**. Submitting it empty is rejected with "Customer demeanor is required."
- What matters most to the customer (for example, cleanliness, air seal, rodents) — **required in the form** ("Customer concerns is required"). The saved record treats this as optional, so the requirement is enforced by the form rather than the stored data.

### Property Details

This section mixes property-level questions (stored on the property) with several report fields.

- Number of stories — **required**.
- The home's total square footage — **required**; a whole number greater than zero.
- The attic's square footage — **required**; a whole number greater than zero.
- The crawlspace's square footage — **required**; a whole number greater than zero.
- An explanation of why the square footage differs between the total home area and each individual space (for example, vaults, two-story layouts, flat roofs, or half crawlspaces) — **required** free text.
- Estimated parking distance from the front door — **required**.
- Whether there are pet concerns the crew must know about — **required**.
- Special instructions for box-truck parking or property access (for example, gate code, lockbox) — **required** free text ("Special instructions is required").

### Walkthrough Notes

- Any visible damage seen during the walkthrough — **required** multi-select. The choices are: no pre-existing damage, ceiling drywall cracks, wall cracks or chips, baseboard chips, floor scratching, light-fixture damage, driveway stains, broken attic lid, broken attic ladder, front-yard damage, and other.
- A free-text description of any damage selected above — optional.

### Roof Information

The roof questions are all **optional** — fill in the ones that apply:

- **Roof material** — asphalt shingles, clay tiles, cement tiles, woodshaker, metal, torch down, rubber, or other.
- **Roof age** (consult with client) — under 5 years, 5–10, 11–20, over 20, or client unsure.
- **Roof condition** — good, fair, or needs replacement.
- **Roof decking type** — plywood, slates, or none.
- **Whether the roof is walkable** — yes or no.
- **Whether the customer wants a full roof inspection** — yes or no.

Roofing photos and videos can be attached in this section. The upload controls are hidden when the report is read-only.

### HVAC Information

- **Customer comfort level** — optional; high, moderate, or low comfort.
- **HVAC age** — optional; same age choices as roof age.
- **HVAC system condition** — optional; good, fair, or needs replacement.
- **Air-ducts condition** — optional; good, fair, or needs replacement.
- **Whether the customer wants a full HVAC or furnace inspection** — optional; yes or no.

HVAC photos and videos can be attached in this section. The upload controls are hidden when the report is read-only.

### Crew Notes

- Free-text notes about what is unique about the job or home (for example, the home layout, tight spaces, hidden attics, or a detail-oriented homeowner) — **required**, and capped at a maximum length. Leaving it empty is rejected with "This field is required," and exceeding the cap with "Crew notes must be 5000 characters or fewer."
- Crew notes have an optional AI assist:
  - **Enhance with AI** appears when notes already exist.
  - **Suggest with AI** appears when notes are empty but other context is present (demeanor, concerns, special instructions).
  - The AI control is unavailable when there is neither notes nor context, and it is hidden entirely in read-only view.
- If AI enhancement fails, an "Enhancement failed" message appears and the notes are left as they were. An applied AI suggestion can be undone.
- Crew notes belong to the estimate, so you can also view or add them outside the report: from the **Crew Notes** column on the project's **Estimates** tab, or from the estimate's menu in the mobile app. Those screens have no AI assist.

## Completion and submission rules

<Card title="Required-field gate" icon="list-check">
  To save the report, the required fields must be filled. If any are missing, **Save Report** shows **Report incomplete** ("Answer the required questions before saving.") and each missing field shows its own message.
</Card>

<Card title="The report saves on its own" icon="floppy-disk">
  The report has its own **Save Report** button and is saved separately from the estimate's pricing. Saving stores the report answers, the crew notes, and the property details together. If the save fails, you see **Unable to save report**.
</Card>

<Card title="When a report counts as complete" icon="circle-check">
  A report is complete when it was saved on the current question set and its required answers are filled in. A report saved on the older question set counts as **Not started** until someone opens it, answers the current questions, and saves it again.
</Card>

<Card title="One report per estimate" icon="link">
  Saving creates the report the first time and updates the same one thereafter. Because it is keyed to the estimate, there is never more than one.
</Card>

<Card title="Locked once the estimate is Sold" icon="lock">
  An existing report cannot be updated once its estimate is in the **Sold** state. The save is rejected with "Cannot update a report with a Sold estimate."
</Card>

<Card title="The estimate must exist" icon="circle-exclamation">
  Saving a report for an estimate that cannot be found (or that is not in the user's organization) is rejected because the estimate can't be found.
</Card>

## Read-only viewing

- A saved report always opens as a read-only document first: **Inspection Report:** followed by the estimate name, the salesperson and date, and a **Print** button. If you can edit it, click **Edit Report** to switch to the form.
- The report stays **read-only** when your role can't edit the estimate, when it's another salesperson's estimate you can't edit, or once the estimate is no longer **In Progress** (for example **Released**, **Sold**, or **Lost**) or its proposal is signed. If nothing was saved yet, it reads "No inspection report has been filled in yet."
- Photos and videos show in the read-only view, without the upload controls.
- Reports saved before the current question set was introduced keep showing their original question set when viewed read-only (including the earlier questions about scheduling factors, special materials, occupancy, obstacles or hazards, and structural features). Opening such a report to edit switches it to the current questions, and saving upgrades it to the current version.
- On a read-only report, the Roof Information and HVAC Information sections are hidden when they hold no answers, so an older report that skipped them is not shown padded with empty sections.
- To make changes to a locked estimate's report, change the estimate's status back to **In Progress** from the status menu next to the proposal's title. On a signed proposal, this starts a change order.

## Quick reference

- **One inspection report per estimate**, captured by the salesperson during the walkthrough.
- **Create or edit:** Admin, Ops Manager, Sales Admin, Sales Member, Client Coordinator. Reports are not deletable in the product.
- **Available only when the inspection-report feature is on**, and only on estimates built from your price book. Open it from the proposal's **⋮** menu → **Inspection Report**.
- **Required before sending:** until it's complete, the proposal can't be sent or presented, and the estimate can't be Released or Sold.
- **Saved on its own** with **Save Report**; required fields are enforced by the form before it saves.
- **Locked once the estimate is Sold**; opens **read-only** for any estimate that is not In Progress or whose proposal is signed.
- Roof and HVAC sections accept **photos and videos**; the upload controls are hidden in read-only view.
