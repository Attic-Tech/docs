If your company is new to Menaia, you can create a workspace yourself in a few minutes, with no credit card. You sign up with your email or Google, tell Menaia about your business, and Meni reads your website to set up a starting workspace for you: a price book, branches, lead sources, tags, follow-ups, and a dashboard. You review what Meni found before it builds.

<Note>
If you don't see **Start free** on the sign-in page, creating a workspace yourself isn't available right now. If your company already uses Menaia, don't create a new workspace. Ask an Admin to add you, then [sign in](/guides/start/sign-in).
</Note>

## 1. Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up page">
    On the sign-in page, press **Start free** (next to "New to Menaia?").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to sign up">
    Enter your **Work email** and press **Start free trial**, or press **Continue with Google**. By continuing, you agree to Menaia's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. You'll see **14-day free trial** and **No credit card required**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    If you used email, the page reads **Check your email** and shows the address it sent to. Type the 6-digit code from the email — it's checked for you as soon as you finish typing. **Resend Code** sends a new one (you can use it again after a minute), and **Back** returns you to the email screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Already have an account? Press **Log in** at the top of the page instead.

## 2. Tell Menaia about your business

The next screen is titled **Tell us about your business** and says "Meni uses your website to set up your workspace next." It shows **Step 1 of 5**.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **First name** and **Last name** | Your name. Filled in from Google if you used it. |
| **Company name** | Your business name. If you signed up with a company email or company Google account, this may already be filled in from your email domain. |
| **Work email** | The email you signed up with. This is shown but can't be changed here. |
| **Website** | Your business web address. Add it so Meni can look up your business. |

When you type your website, Meni looks it up and shows what it found:

- **Found** — a card with your business name, type, city, rating, and web address. If it isn't yours, press **Not right?**, then pick your **Trade** and add your locations (one branch is created for each), and press **Update** — or press **Actually, that was us** to keep the match.
- **Not found** — "We couldn't find \<your website\> on Google. Continue and Meni will use what you entered."

Press **Continue**. The button reads **Setting up your workspace...** while Menaia creates your workspace, makes you its Admin, and starts building. Then setup opens with Meni.

<Note>
Signing up with an email that's already part of a Menaia workspace shows **You already have a workspace**: "This email belongs to an existing Menaia workspace, so there is nothing to set up here." Press **Log in to your workspace**. If your workspace was already created, Menaia shows **Opening your workspace…** and takes you straight in.
</Note>

## 3. Review what Meni found

Setup opens with **Hi \<your name\>, I'm Meni - Menaia's built-in AI** (**Step 2 of 5**). If you added a website, Meni says "Here's what I found about \<your company\>. Take a look, and add or fix anything I missed before we set up your workspace." Without a website, Meni starts with common details for your trade and location.

Meni shows a card you can review and edit. Press **Edit** on any row to change it, then save:

- **Services** — the services Meni found. These become your price book items.
- **Branch locations** — the towns Meni found. A branch is created for each one.
- **Reputation** — your Google rating and review count, when Meni finds them.
- **Working days & hours** — used as your working hours for bookings.

When the card looks right, press **Continue**.

## 4. Add your lead sources

**Where do your leads come from?** (**Step 3 of 5**) lets you pick where your leads come from, so you'll see the source on every lead. Choose any that apply: **Google Business Profile**, **Referrals**, **Website Form**, **Phone Calls**, **Angi**, **Thumbtack**, **Yelp**, **Facebook**, **Home Shows**, or **Other** (name it yourself). You can change these anytime in Settings.

Press **Continue**, or press **Skip** to move on without choosing.

## 5. Invite your team

**Invite your team** (**Step 4 of 5**) says "Choose a role for each person so they see just what they need. Your trial includes 5 seats. You can always invite more people later in Settings."

- Enter each person's email and choose their role.
- Press **Add more** to add another person, up to 5.
- Press **Send Invites**, or press **Skip** to invite people later.

## 6. Watch Meni build

The last screen (**Step 5 of 5**) shows **Building your workspace** with a progress bar and a live checklist:

- Reading your website (or **Learning about your business** if you didn't add one)
- Adding your services
- Setting up your lead sources and tags
- Drafting your follow-ups
- Your workspace is ready

When it's done, you'll see **Welcome aboard, \<your name\>. Your workspace is ready!** and your **Dashboard** opens.

<Note>
On a wide screen, a side panel follows along as you go. **Your workspace** counts what's been built (price book, lead sources, tags, follow-ups, and team & roles), and **Meni's notes** fills in what Meni learns about your business.
</Note>

## If setup doesn't finish

If something goes wrong while Meni is building, you'll see **That didn't finish**: "Something went wrong while I was building. Your progress is saved — we can try again or start you on the standard setup."

- **Try again** runs the build again with the answers you already gave.
- **Use the standard setup** skips the rest and gives you a starter workspace: a starter price book for your trade with no prices filled in, starter lead sources, tags, and follow-ups, your first dashboard, and standard notification rules, payment terms, and invoice credit and void reasons.

## Sign-up messages

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "That code expired or is incorrect. Request a new one." | Press **Resend Code** and enter the new code. |
| "That code is not valid. Check it and try again." | Re-enter the code, or request a new one. |
| "Too many attempts. Wait a few minutes, then try again." | Wait a few minutes before trying the code again. |
| "We could not send you a code. Please try again in a minute." | Wait a minute, then press **Start free trial** again. |
| "This email already belongs to an existing Menaia workspace. Log in instead." | Sign in to your existing workspace instead. |
| "Signup is not open right now." | Creating a workspace yourself isn't available at the moment. |
| "Too many attempts. Wait a minute and try again." | Wait a minute, then press **Continue** again. |
| "We could not create your workspace. Please try again." | Press **Continue** again. |

## What gets created

When Meni builds your workspace, Menaia sets up:

- **Your business profile** — your website, address, and phone from your Google listing or site. Each location Meni found becomes its own branch.
- **Your price book** — the services Meni found on your website or Google listing, or common services for your trade, with their categories, work areas, factors (multipliers for tougher situations), and additional costs (flat fees like trip charges).
- **Lead sources and tags** from your answers.
- **Follow-ups** — starter automations Meni drafts for you to review.
- **Standard defaults** — notification rules, payment terms, and invoice credit and void reasons, and an invoice footer with your company name.
- **Meni** — turned on for your workspace, with your first **Dashboard** pinned.
- **Team invites** — sent to anyone you added.

## What to do next

After setup, a small progress circle in the bottom-right corner of your screen opens the **Set up your workspace** guide. It shows how many steps you've finished and links you to each next step:

- **Build your price book**
- **Set up your phone line** (not on every plan)
- **Add your first lead**
- **Send your first estimate**
- **Book your first job**
- **Invite a teammate**

The guide also holds **Meni's plan for your business**. To hide it, press **Don't show this again**. When every step is done, press **Dismiss guide**.
