Menaia keeps everything in one consistent layout: a **sidebar** on the left to move between areas, and a main workspace that fills with whatever you're working on. Once you know how the sidebar is organized, you can find anything in a couple of clicks.

<Note>
Your sidebar only shows the areas your role can use, so it may be shorter than what you see here. That's normal — Menaia hides what you don't need.
</Note>

## Your home page: My Performance

When you log in, most people land on **My Performance** — your personal performance dashboard. It's a quick read on how things are going, with results, leaderboards, and trends for you and your team. If your company runs more than one branch, a **Branch / Company** toggle lets you switch between one location and the whole organization, and you can change the time period to see today, this week, or longer.

If your role doesn't have My Performance, you land on the first area your role uses instead — for example **Leads**.

<Tip>
Think of My Performance as your morning check-in: open it first to see where things stand before diving into the day's work. If Meni is turned on for you, the **Dashboard** is a second place to check in — see below.
</Tip>

## The sidebar, area by area

The sidebar groups related tools together so you can find them fast.

### Main

Your personal, everyday tools.

- **My Performance** — your dashboard of results and trends.
- **Dashboard** — Meni's home: what needs your attention, your morning briefing, drafts waiting for your approval, and the charts you've pinned from Meni. A number next to it shows how many drafts are waiting for you. See [Meni Dashboard](/guides/meni/dashboard).
- **Automations** — set up playbooks and watch rules so Meni drafts follow-up work for your approval. See [Automations](/guides/meni/automations).
- **Communications** — text and call your clients from one inbox. A number next to it shows your unread conversations.
- **Call Center** — a live view of the call queue, your agents, recent activity, and call and text volume.
- **Schedule** — the calendar of upcoming and assigned work.
- **My Wallet** — your earnings and pay-related details.

<Note>
**Dashboard** and **Automations** appear only when Meni and its automations are turned on for your workspace, and not for every role — Client Coordinators don't have them. **Communications** and **Call Center** appear when Communications is turned on for your workspace; Crew Members don't have them. See [Feature availability](/reference/platform/feature-availability).
</Note>

### Sales

Everything for winning new work.

- **Leads** — new inquiries and potential customers.
- **Clients** — your established customers and their properties.
- **T&M Calculator** — a quick time-and-materials pricing tool.
- **Ride-Alongs** — capture and review field visits.
- **Estimates** — build, find, and manage itemized estimates.

### Jobs

Everything for getting the work done.

- **Jobs** — sold work to schedule, assign, and complete.
- **Shifts** — track the crew's time on the job.

### Admin

Company-wide oversight and setup (visible to managers and admins).

- **AI BI** — a built-in set of live charts and reports about your business.
- **Bonus Report** — track and reward team performance.
- **Invoices** — bill completed work and follow payments.
- **Integrations** — connect Menaia to the other tools you use.
- **Settings** — manage your workspace, team, and preferences.

## Meni, your AI assistant

**Meni** is available from the top bar on every page. Click the **sparkles** button, or press **Cmd + J** (Mac) or **Ctrl + J** (Windows), to open the Meni panel and ask a question — how to do something in Menaia, or, when the full assistant is on for you, anything about your leads, jobs, schedule, and more. The **question-mark** button next to it opens Meni to report a problem.

See [Ask Meni](/guides/meni/ask-meni) for everything Meni can do.

## Settings: tailoring your workspace

The **Settings** area (under Admin) is where your workspace is shaped to fit how your company works — your team and their access, branch details, pricing, and the preferences that drive the rest of the product. You'll usually visit Settings to set things up or adjust them, not for day-to-day work.

<Tip>
If something in Menaia behaves differently than a teammate's — like a step that appears for them but not you — it's often a **branch setting**. Settings is where those differences are configured.
</Tip>

## Finding things fast with search

Most list pages — **Leads**, **Clients**, **Estimates**, **Jobs**, **Invoices** — give you a **search box** and **filters** at the top. Type a name, address, or keyword to narrow a long list down to what you're after, and use filters to focus on a status, a person, or a time range.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  OPEN[Open a list page] --> SEARCH[Type in the search box]
  SEARCH --> FILTER[Apply filters]
  FILTER --> FOUND[Open exactly what you need]
```

<Tip>
Looking for one specific customer? Start in **Clients** and search their name — from there you can reach their properties, estimates, and jobs all in one place.
</Tip>
