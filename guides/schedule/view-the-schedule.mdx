The **Schedule** is your team's calendar. It shows everything that's been scheduled — jobs, lead activities, tasks, and time off — laid out by day and by person. This page walks you through opening the Schedule and getting it to show exactly the slice you want to see.

<Note>
You don't change anything just by looking. Switching views, grouping, or jumping to a different date only changes how the calendar is displayed — it never moves or edits what's on it.
</Note>

## Open the Schedule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Schedule">
    Choose **Schedule** from the main navigation. The calendar opens to the current period with today already in view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your bearings">
    The controls live in a bar across the top. On the left you'll find **Today** and the date arrows; on the right you'll find the **Mode** and **View** menus and the **Filters** button. The calendar fills the rest of the screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose how much you see: Day, Week, or Month

Use the view menu (the one showing **Day**, **Week**, or **Month**) on the right side of the top bar to change the span of time on screen.

- **Day** — a single day in detail. Best when a day is busy and you want to see each event clearly.
- **Week** — the standard view. A full week side by side, so you can spot gaps and overlaps at a glance.
- **Month** — the big picture. A whole month, handy for planning further out.

<Tip>
Start in **Week** for everyday work and drop into **Day** when a particular day looks packed.
</Tip>

## Choose a mode: User, Job, Phase, or Calendar

Next to the view menu is the **Mode** switcher, which changes how the calendar is organized. It offers **User**, **Job**, **Phase**, and **Calendar**.

- **User** lays the calendar out one row per person, so you can see each team member's day. Active subcontractors get their own rows too, shown with a **Subcontractor** label under the name — so a job staffed only by a subcontractor still appears. A subcontractor's row holds jobs only, not tasks or time off. If you can see the whole team's schedule, an **Unassigned** row sits at the top, gathering jobs, tasks, and lead activities that have nobody assigned yet — handy for spotting work that still needs an owner. The Unassigned row never holds time off, which always belongs to someone.
- **Job** groups events by the job they belong to, and shows the assigned people — including any subcontractors — as small avatars on each event, useful when you care more about "who's on this job" than "what is this person doing."
- **Phase** builds on **Job**: in the **Day** and **Week** views it splits each job's shifts into a row per phase (for example *Prep*, *Install*, *Cleanup*), each phase in its own colour, so you can see how a multi-phase job is spread across the days. In the **Month** view, Phase lays out the same as Job.
- **Calendar** draws the hours of the day down the left edge and places each event at its real start and end time, like a classic day or week calendar. It's available in the **Day** and **Week** views; in **Month**, the calendar falls back to the standard month layout.

Pick whichever matches the question you're trying to answer.

<Note>
Someone who's been **blocked** still keeps a row while they have past work in view, so their history stays readable — their name shows marked **(blocked)**. That row is read-only: you can open its existing jobs, tasks, and time off, but you can't schedule anything new onto a blocked person.
</Note>

## Move around in time

- **Today** snaps the calendar back to the current period — your quickest way home after browsing around.
- The **left and right arrows** step backward and forward one Day, Week, or Month at a time, depending on the view you're in.
- The label between the arrows tells you exactly which range you're looking at.

## Read the calendar

Everything on the calendar is a colored card. The color tells you the activity type, and the card itself shows the essentials — usually the client or title and the time range. Exactly which other fields a card shows is set per branch by an admin (see [Choose what cards show](#choose-what-cards-show-admins)); the examples below describe the out-of-the-box fields.

- **Jobs** show the client and job, plus assigned people (as avatars) in Job grouping.
- **Lead Activities** show the lead's name. A green check means the customer has confirmed the visit, a flame icon marks a hot (high-priority) lead, and a shuffle icon marks a flexible client.
- **Tasks** show the task title or type.
- **Time Off** shows the reason and the time range.

A small **eye-off icon** on a card means it's unpublished — it's been drafted but the team hasn't been notified yet. In a multi-branch setup, a small colored pill on a card names the branch it belongs to.

Select any card to open its details, where you can review or edit it.

<Note>
If nothing is scheduled for the range you're viewing, you'll see **Nothing scheduled** along with a **Create New** button so you can add something right away.
</Note>

## Choose what cards show (admins)

The fields on each calendar card aren't fixed — an **Admin** can choose them for the whole branch. The gear icon (**Calendar settings**) next to **Filters** opens a panel where you pick, separately for **Jobs**, **Tasks**, and **Lead Activities**, which fields appear on their cards. The card's title is always shown and isn't one of the choices.

For example, Job cards can show the phase and day, time, crew, crew lead, address, ZIP code, job name and number, shift notes, lead source, job status, or client phone; Task and Lead Activity cards have their own matching sets.

Cards show the chosen fields in a fixed order and leave off the last ones when there isn't room on a small card — hover a card to see all of them. Leaving a type's fields empty shows just the title. Click **Save** to apply the change for everyone in the branch; these settings are per branch, so each branch can show different fields.

## Narrow it down with Filters

When the calendar is crowded, the **Filters** button opens a panel where you can limit what shows. You can filter by **People** (team members and subcontractors together — plus an **Unassigned** option, if you can see the whole team's schedule, to show only work nobody is assigned to), **Clients**, **Role**, **Activity Types** (Jobs, Lead Activities, Tasks, Time-Off), and **Status** (Published or Unpublished). In the **Job** and **Phase** modes, a **Jobs** filter also appears, so you can narrow the calendar to specific jobs. A number next to the **Filters** button tells you how many filters are currently on, and **Clear Filters** resets them.

If you find yourself setting the same filters again and again, you can save them as a reusable view.

<Tip>
For the full walkthrough of saving and reusing filter sets, see [Filter and save views](/guides/schedule/filter-and-save-views).
</Tip>

## Add something from the Schedule

The **Create New** button in the top-right corner lets you add to the calendar without leaving it. It opens a short menu with **Lead Activity**, **Job**, **Task**, and **Time Off**. You can also click an empty time slot directly on the calendar to start creating in that spot.
