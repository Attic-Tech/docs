---
title: "Enhance Crew Notes with AI"
description: "In the estimate's inspection report, turn quick, rough crew notes into clear instructions the crew can use — or have AI draft them for you — with one tap."
icon: "pen-to-square"
---

**Crew Notes** are where you capture anything the field team should know before they arrive — the home layout, tight spaces, hidden attics, a detail-oriented homeowner, and other "don't forget this" reminders. In the inspection report, AI can clean up and round out those notes for you so nothing important gets lost in shorthand.

<Note>
The AI button is part of the estimate's **Inspection Report**, so it's available only if inspection reports are turned on for your workspace, on estimates built from your price book. You also need to be able to edit the report: the estimate must be **In Progress** with an unsigned proposal, and it must be yours unless you're a manager.
</Note>

## Open the Crew Notes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the inspection report">
    On the estimate's proposal page, click **⋮** at the top and choose **Inspection Report**. In the mobile app, tap **⋮** on the proposal and choose **Inspection Report**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to editing">
    If the report was already saved, it opens read-only. Click **Edit Report** to change it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Crew Notes">
    Scroll to the **Crew Notes** section at the end of the report. The question asks *what is unique about this job or home*.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enhance or draft your notes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Jot down your notes">
    Type whatever you've got — even rough, half-finished notes are fine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the AI button">
    A **sparkle** button sits in the notes box. When you already have notes, it's **Enhance with AI** — click it to polish them. If the box is empty but you've answered the customer's demeanor, their main concerns, or the special instructions, it's **Suggest with AI** and drafts notes from those answers instead. On the web, hover the button to see its name; in the mobile app the name shows on the button. With no notes and none of those answers filled in, the button doesn't appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait a moment">
    The button spins while the AI works. When it's done, your notes are replaced with the new version, right in the box. If something goes wrong, you'll see **Enhancement failed** and your notes stay as they were.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the report">
    Click **Save Report**. The new notes aren't saved until you do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep it or undo it

After the AI updates your notes, two controls appear next to the sparkle:

- **Keep** (the checkmark) — you're happy with the result. The new text stays in place.
- **Revert** (the ×) — put your original notes back exactly as you wrote them.

In the mobile app, these are **Dismiss** and **Undo**.

<Tip>
The AI writes better notes when it has more to work with. Answer the customer's demeanor, their main concerns, and any special instructions in the report first.
</Tip>

## Other places to edit crew notes

You can also view and edit an estimate's crew notes without AI:

- **On the web**, open the project's **Estimates** tab and click **View Notes** or **Add Notes** in the **Crew Notes** column.
- **In the mobile app**, tap **⋮** on the estimate and choose **View Crew Notes** or **Add Notes**.

<Note>
Crew notes stay editable from these places even after an estimate is marked Sold or moved out of In Progress — they're an annotation for the team, not part of the price.
</Note>
