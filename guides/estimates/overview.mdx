The **Estimates** area is where you price jobs and follow them all the way to a "yes." You build each estimate right on its proposal — the same page your customer will see — by adding work areas and items from your price book, or by typing in your own line items. Then you track and update every estimate from one list. Along the way, AI helps sharpen your work: it polishes the notes you leave for the crew, and it coaches you on recorded ride-alongs.

<Note>
You don't need to know any pricing math. For price-book estimates, Menaia handles the numbers — your job is to describe the work accurately.
</Note>
