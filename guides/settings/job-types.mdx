**Job types** are the kinds of work you do — repairs, installs, inspections, and so on. Your team sets one on a lead, a project made from that lead carries it over, and you can sort and filter leads, jobs, and the Schedule by it. This page is where you define the list your team picks from.

<Note>
Managing job types is an **Admin** task. You'll find it under **Settings**, and only team members who can manage workspace settings can open it. Everyone else just picks from the list.
</Note>

## Open Job Types

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Open **Settings** from the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Leads">
    Under **Sales & Operations**, choose **Leads**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Job Types">
    Click the **Job Types** tab. You can also type "job types" or "service types" in the settings search to jump straight here.
  </Step>
</Steps>

New workspaces start with a list of job types for their trade, so you'll usually see a few already. If the list is empty, you'll see **No job types yet** with a **New job type** button.

## Read the list

Each row shows:

- **Name** — the label your team sees in the **Job type** picker.
- **Order** — where the job type sits in every job type list. Lower numbers come first, starting at 0.
- **Status** — an **Active** or **Inactive** badge.

Use the **Search by name...** box to find one, and click a column heading to sort. On a phone, each job type appears as a card with a menu for **Edit**.

## Add a job type

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new job type">
    Click **Create New** at the top of the Leads settings and choose **Job Type** (or **New job type** when the list is empty).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Name** — for example, *AC Repair* — and click **Create**. New job types start active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit, reorder, or switch off a job type

Click the pencil on a row to open **Edit Job Type**. There you can:

- Change the **Name**.
- Change the **Order** to move it up or down every job type list.
- Clear **Is Active** to stop offering it.

Click **Save** when you're done.

<Note>
Job types can't be deleted, only switched off. An inactive job type disappears from the **Job type** picker, but leads, projects, and jobs that already use it keep it.
</Note>

## Where job types show up

- **Leads** — set an optional **Job type** when you [create a lead](/guides/leads/create-a-lead), including from the Schedule.
- **Projects and jobs** — a project made from a lead carries its job type over, and its jobs show it.
- **Filters** — filter the [Schedule](/guides/schedule/filter-and-save-views), leads, and jobs by job type.
