**Communications** is where you set up your business phone in Menaia. You get a phone number (or bring one you already own), decide who each line rings, and choose what callers hear when they call in. Your team then calls and texts from the [inbox](/guides/calls/inbox) and the [dialer](/guides/calls/use-the-dialer).

<Note>
Communications is part of Settings, which only **Admins** can open. To find it, open **Settings** from the main navigation and choose **Communications** under **General**.
</Note>

The page has four parts, from top to bottom:

1. **Lines**: every number your workspace has. Click a line to open its own settings.
2. **Carrier registrations**: where your texting and caller ID stand with the phone carriers.
3. **Port a number**: bring a number over from another carrier.
4. **Workspace call settings**: greeting, phone menu, business hours, voicemail, forwarding, ringing, recording, and after-hours handling. These apply to every call your workspace receives.

## Lines

The **Lines** card lists every number your workspace has, along with its branch and who answers it. Each row shows:

| Part of the row | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Name | The line's **Inbox name**. If it has none, you see the number instead. |
| Number | Shown next to the name when the line has an inbox name. |
| **Local** or **Toll-free** | The type of number. |
| **Primary** | This is the main number for its branch. |
| Status | **Provisioning**, **Active**, **Error**, or **Released** (see below). |
| People | Up to four names, then "+N more". If nobody is listed, you see "No one is on this line yet". |
| Branch | The branch the line belongs to. Hidden if the line has no branch. |

Line statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Provisioning** | The number is still being set up. |
| **Active** | The number is working and can take and make calls. |
| **Error** | Something went wrong while setting the number up. |
| **Released** | The number no longer belongs to your workspace. You can't choose a released line for masking or add people to it. |

Click anywhere on a row to open that line's settings.

### Set up your phone

The first time your workspace sets up its phone, the **Lines** card is empty and shows **Set up your phone**. This opens a short wizard that gets you a number for calls and files everything for texting with the phone carriers in one place. You answer the questions once, for both calls and texts. Nothing is bought or filed until you submit the last step, and your answers are saved as you go — if you leave partway through, the **Lines** card shows **Continue setup** so you can pick up where you left off.

<Note>
Only an **Admin** can set up the phone, buy numbers, or file texting. Everyone else sees "Ask an admin to set up your phone."
</Note>

The wizard has these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your business">
    The carriers check these against your business tax records, so enter them exactly as they appear there. Fill in:

    - **Legal business name** and **EIN or tax ID** (required unless you're a sole proprietor)
    - **Business website**, **Business type**, and **Industry**
    - **Name on caller ID** — what customers see when you call them. Up to 15 letters, numbers, and spaces; phones show it in capital letters.
    - **Business address** (street, city, state, and ZIP)
    - A contact the carriers can reach: **Contact name**, **Phone number**, and **Email**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your numbers">
    You get a **local number** that makes and receives calls from day one and becomes your main texting line once the carriers approve it, usually in 10 to 15 days. Search by area code or city and pick one; it's held for you for 15 minutes.

    You can also turn on **Include a toll-free number**. A toll-free number has its own, faster texting review, so you can text customers within a few days while your local number waits. It's off by default, and Menaia picks the toll-free number for you when you submit — you can always add one later. A timeline on this step shows when each number starts calling and texting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fees">
    When this step is shown, it lists what your numbers and texting cost each month and what's due when you submit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    See everything that will be filed — your business details, your numbers, and your caller ID name — along with **What we do when you submit** and the setup fees. Click **Edit** on any part to change it, then **Submit and buy numbers**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After you submit, a progress screen shows where each part stands. You can leave the page — setup keeps going, and you're told when texting is on. Your numbers make and receive calls as soon as they're bought, while the carriers review texting. To follow the review afterwards, see [Carrier registrations](#carrier-registrations).

New numbers start without a branch. The first number your workspace gets becomes **Primary** automatically. To assign a branch, open the line and go to the **General** tab.

### Add another number

Once your workspace is set up, the **Lines** card shows **Add number** instead. This opens a quick window — there's no second application to fill in:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the kind of number">
    Choose **Local number** or **Toll-free**. The window shows what each can do right away and its monthly cost.
    - A **local number** joins your existing local registration. If that registration is approved it can text right away; otherwise it makes and receives calls now and texts once the registration clears.
    - A **toll-free** number gets its own texting registration, filed with your business details, and can text once the carriers approve it, usually in a few days. If Menaia doesn't have the business details a toll-free number needs yet, the window asks for them before you buy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a number">
    Search by area code or city for a local number, or pick from the available toll-free numbers. Numbers are held for you for 15 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy the number">
    Click **Buy number** and confirm. You see "Number added and ready to use", and the number appears on the **Lines** card ready to call right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Add number** is unavailable while your workspace's first number is still being bought — it becomes available once that number is set up.
</Note>

## Carrier registrations

The **Carrier registrations** card shows where your texting and caller ID stand with the phone carriers. It appears once your workspace has set up its phone. Each registration gets its own lane:

- **Texting from your toll-free number** — one lane per toll-free number, each reviewed on its own.
- **Texting from your local numbers** — the one registration that covers all your local numbers.
- **Caller ID on all your numbers** — the name customers see when you call them.

Each lane shows a status badge and a short explanation of where it stands:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Setting up** | Menaia is buying your number and preparing its registration. |
| **Under review** | The carriers are reviewing your details. A local registration usually takes 10 to 15 days; a toll-free one, a few days. Your numbers make and receive calls the whole time. |
| **Approved** | Your workspace can send and receive texts from the numbers this registration covers. |
| **Not approved** | The carriers didn't approve the registration. Your numbers still make and receive calls. |
| **Needs attention** | Something went wrong before the registration reached the carriers. |

When a lane needs something from you, it shows the one action to take:

- **Review and file again** on a toll-free registration the carriers couldn't confirm. You correct the details they flagged and file that number's registration again. There's a limit on how many times you can file, and the lane shows how many filings are left and when you can file next. Your corrections apply only to that number's registration; registrations that are already approved aren't filed again.
- **Register local numbers** if your workspace started on a toll-free number and hasn't registered its local numbers for texting yet. This reuses your business details and files the local registration.
- **Choose your caller ID name** (or **Change name**) to set or update the name customers see when you call. The carriers review it, and phones start showing it 2 to 3 days after it's approved.
- **Contact support** when a registration wasn't approved or needs attention, so the team can go through it with you and file it again.

Only an **Admin** sees these actions. Other roles see a note pointing them to an admin, for example "Ask an admin to register your local numbers."

## Line settings

Click a line to open its page. At the top you see the line's name with its badges, plus its number and branch. Three buttons sit next to them:

- **Back to Communications** returns you to the main page.
- **Open inbox** takes you to the [inbox](/guides/calls/inbox).
- **Test call** calls the line from your dialer.

The line page has five tabs: **General**, **People**, **Business hours**, **Call flow**, and **Call handling**.

### General

Each change saves when you leave the field, and a message confirms it saved.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Inbox name** | What your team sees in the inbox for this line, for example "Front desk". Leave it blank to show the number. |
| **Branch** | The branch this line belongs to. Only people in this branch can be added on the **People** tab. Choose **No branch** to unassign. |
| **Caller ID name** | What customers see when you call them. |
| **Primary number of the branch** | New conversations start from this number. When you turn it on for one line, it turns off on any other line in the same branch, because each branch has one primary number. |

### People

**People on this number** lists who works this line. Every setting on this tab saves as soon as you change it.

To add someone, click **Add person or role** and pick from either list:

- **Roles**: every person in that role is on the line, and anyone added to the role later is included too. Each role shows how many people in the branch have it.
- **People**: only people in the line's branch appear. If everyone is already on the line, you see "Everyone in *branch* is already on this number".

Tick what each person or role can do on this number. Each row has these columns:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Answer** | This person can answer calls to the line. |
| **Calls** | This person can take and place calls on the line. |
| **Text** | This person can text from the line. |
| **Voicemail** | This person can see the line's voicemail. |
| **Recordings** | Marks this person for access to the line's call recordings. Recordings are kept, but there's no place to play them back in Menaia yet. |
| **Masked** | This person's personal number stays hidden, in both directions (see below). |
| **Ring order** | This row's place in line when a call comes in. |
| **Remove** | Takes this person or role off the line. |

**Masked** hides personal numbers both ways. When a masked person calls a client from a job, the client sees the line's masking number instead of the person's cell, and the masked person never sees the client's cell either. Turn it on for a role and everyone in that role is masked. A masked person has no inbox, so **Answer**, **Calls**, **Text**, and **Voicemail** switch off and lock. Only **Recordings** stays available. You also can't set a ring order for a masked row. To choose which number clients see and whether masked calls are recorded, go to [Call handling](#call-handling).

#### How ringing works

When someone calls a line that has people on it, Menaia rings down the **Ring order**:

- **One slot at a time.** Each row is one slot. If nobody picks up, the call moves to the next slot. The ring time for each slot is **Wait before forwarding**, found under [Ring behaviour](#ring-behaviour).
- **A role rings as a group.** A role's slot rings everyone in that role at the same time. If the line has a branch, only people in that branch are rung.
- **Only some rows ring.** A row rings only if **Answer** and **Calls** are both on and **Masked** is off.
- **Some people are skipped.** Anyone who has set themselves unavailable is skipped. Nobody is rung twice, even if they're on the line both by name and through a role. At most 10 people are rung per call.
- **Nobody answers.** The call goes to your queue (if **Hold callers in a queue** is on) or to voicemail.
- **Nobody is available.** If the line has people who could ring but none of them are available, Menaia doesn't fall back to anyone else. The caller is held in the queue if the queue is on. Otherwise they hear that no one is available and the call ends.

<Note>
If a line has nobody who can ring (no people at all, or no row with **Answer** and **Calls** on and **Masked** off), calls ring your workspace's **Client Coordinators** instead. The line's branch coordinators ring first, and if the branch has none, coordinators across the workspace ring. The names on the **Lines** card come from this same group.
</Note>

### Business hours

Set **Opens** and **Closes** for each day, or tick **Closed**. Then click **Save Changes**. This schedule belongs to the line and is separate from your workspace business hours.

### Call flow

This tab shows **Call flow is coming soon**. It's where visual call routing (phone menus, greetings, and after-hours handling) will live for each line.

### Call handling

This tab has three cards.

**Call masking** applies to everyone marked **Masked** on the **People** tab. These settings save as soon as you change them.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **What the client sees** | The number that shows on the client's phone instead of a personal one. You can choose any of your workspace's numbers that isn't released. It starts as the line's own number. |
| **Record masked calls** | Calls placed by masked people on this line are recorded automatically. |
| **Masked texts** | Texts between a masked person and a client run through the same number. |

**Greeting** holds the message callers hear when they first reach the line. Type it into **Greeting message** and click **Save Changes**.

**Blocked Numbers** has a switch that turns number blocking on or off for the line. The list below it is shared across your whole workspace, so a number you add here also appears on the Communications page. See [Blocked numbers](#blocked-numbers).

<Note>
Right now, incoming calls follow your **workspace** greeting, business hours, and blocked-number switch, not the ones on a line's tabs. The same goes for masked texts: the per-line setting is saved but texting doesn't use it yet. The line-level versions are saved and are meant to work with **Call flow** once it arrives. **What the client sees** and **Record masked calls** already apply to outgoing calls.
</Note>

## Workspace call settings

Below the **Port a number** card, a set of cards controls how your workspace handles calls. Change what you need, then click **Save Changes** at the bottom. The **Blocked Numbers** switch and list are the exception, since they save immediately.

The cards appear in this order.

### Greeting

**Greeting message** is what callers hear when they first reach you. It plays together with the phone menu, so callers hear it when the phone menu is on.

### Blocked numbers

Use this to block specific numbers from calling your business. Blocked callers hear a busy signal.

- Use the switch in the card header to turn blocking on or off.
- To block a number, click **Add Number**, enter the **Phone number** and an optional **Label/reason** (for example "Telemarketer"), then click **Block Number**.
- The list shows each **Phone Number**, its **Label/Reason**, and the **Date Added**. Click the trash icon to unblock a number.

### Phone menu (IVR)

A phone menu lets callers press a key to reach the right place. Tick **Enable the phone menu**, then click **Add menu option** for each key. Each option has:

- **Digit**: a key from 0 to 9. Each digit can be used once.
- **Action**: what happens when the caller presses that key. See the table below.
- **Label**: a name for the option, for example "Sales".

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Ring specific users** | Rings the people you pick in **Ring users**. From the phone menu, only Client Coordinators are rung, so pick from them. |
| **Ring everyone** | Rings your Client Coordinators: the branch's coordinators first, or everyone in the role if the branch has none. |
| **Send to voicemail** | Sends the caller straight to voicemail. |
| **Play a message** | Plays the **Message** you write, then ends the call. |

### Business hours

Choose a **Timezone**, then set **Opens** and **Closes** for each day or tick **Closed**.

<Note>
Business hours and the **After-hours behavior** card are checked when the phone menu is on. Callers who reach you outside your hours get the after-hours treatment instead of the menu. When the phone menu is off, calls ring the same way at any hour.
</Note>

### Voicemail

Tick **Enable voicemail** and write a **Voicemail greeting**. When nobody answers, callers hear the greeting and can leave a message. If voicemail is off, they hear that no one is available and the call ends.

### Missed-call auto-text

Turn this on to send a text back to callers you miss, so you don't lose the lead. Write the message in **Message template**. It can be up to 640 characters, and the counter shows your length against a single 160-character text. You can't save with the switch on and the message empty.

<Warning>
The template has buttons that insert **Caller first name**, **Business name**, **Booking link**, and **Missed-call time** placeholders. These placeholders aren't filled in when the text goes out yet. For now, write a message that reads well exactly as typed.
</Warning>

### Forward to phone

Turn this on to send business calls to a cell phone, so calls still get answered when nobody is at a desk. Enter the number in **Forward to**. You can't save with the switch on and no number. Next to the number you'll see **Verified** once your workspace has an active business number. Because forwarding is a calling feature, it doesn't wait on carrier text-message registration — as soon as a line is active you can forward calls, even while that number's registration is still under carrier review. Until a line is active, you'll see "Not verified — activate a business number to forward calls".

**If the forwarded call isn't answered either** offers **Send to business voicemail** and **Hand to the AI receptionist**.

<Note>
When forwarding is on and a call doesn't go through the phone menu, it goes straight to the forwarding number. If nobody answers there, the caller goes to your queue (if it's on) or to business voicemail. That happens whichever option you pick above.
</Note>

### Ring behaviour

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Ring strategy** | Choose **Ring the app and the phone together** or **Only forward if nobody answers in the app**. |
| **Wait before forwarding** | How long each ring attempt lasts before the call moves on, from 5 to 45 seconds. On lines with people, this is the ring time for each slot in the ring order. |
| **Hold callers in a queue** | When everyone is busy or unavailable, callers wait on hold and are connected to the next free person instead of going straight to voicemail. |
| **Maximum wait time** | Appears when the queue is on. Set it from 30 seconds to 30 minutes. After this long on hold, the caller goes to voicemail. |

### Incoming and outgoing call recording

These are two separate cards. Turn one on to record every call in that direction automatically, with no action needed from your team.

It's best practice, and required in some regions, to tell people a call is recorded. Each card has a **Custom message (optional)** box for this:

- **Incoming**: callers hear the message as soon as they call in.
- **Outgoing**: the person you call hears it when they answer, before they're connected.

If you leave the box blank, callers hear the default message: "This call may be recorded for quality and training purposes."

### After-hours behavior

This card sets what happens when someone calls outside your business hours. Under **Behavior**, choose one:

- **Send to voicemail**
- **Play a message**, which plays the **After-hours message** you write and then ends the call.

## Port a number

Use **Port a number** to bring a number you already own over from your current carrier. This card only appears if Communications is turned on for your workspace.

<Note>
The **Port a number** button stays disabled until texting is turned on for your workspace — that is, until you've [set up your phone](#set-up-your-phone). A ported number follows the kind of number it is: a ported local number joins your registration for local numbers, and a ported toll-free number gets its own texting review. Until texting is turned on, the card shows "Porting unlocks once texting is turned on. A ported local number joins your registration for local numbers, and a ported toll-free number gets its own texting review."
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the port window">
    On the **Port a number** card, click **Port a number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check each number">
    Under **Numbers to port**, enter a number and click **Check**.
    - If it can be moved, you see "*number* can be ported". Click **Add to request**.
    - If it can't, you see "*number* cannot be ported." followed by the reason. When no reason is given, you see "The current carrier does not support transfers."

    Repeat for every number you want to move. Remove one with its **X** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current carrier details">
    These must match your current carrier's records exactly.
    - **Carrier name**, **Account number**, **Authorized contact name** (the full name on the account), and **Service address** (the address on file with the carrier) are required.
    - **Transfer PIN** is optional unless a number's carrier requires one. In that case it's required, and the field says "Your current carrier requires a PIN to release this number".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload documents">
    Upload a **Signed letter of authorization (LOA)** and a **Recent carrier bill**. Both are required. Each must be a PDF, JPEG, or PNG up to 10 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit port request**. You see "Port request submitted" and the request appears on the card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each request on the card shows its numbers, a status, and the date it was submitted.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Submitted** | Your request has been sent to the carrier. |
| **In progress** | The transfer is underway. |
| **Completed** | The number has moved. It appears on the **Lines** card as an **Active** line. |
| **Rejected** | The transfer was refused. The reason appears under the request as "Rejected: *reason*". |
| **Canceled** | The request was canceled. |

A ported number starts without a branch, and it becomes **Primary** if it's your workspace's first number. To assign it a branch, open the line and go to the **General** tab.

## Which line outgoing calls use

When someone places a call from anywhere in Menaia, the call goes out on one of your active lines. Menaia picks the line in this order:

1. The line the conversation is tied to, if the caller is on that line.
2. Otherwise, the caller's own line. If they're on several, their **Primary** line wins, and if none of those is primary, the one added first does.
3. If the caller isn't on any line, the workspace's primary line, or the active line added first if there's no primary.

Being on a line through a role counts. If the caller is **Masked** on the chosen line, the client sees the line's masking number, and the call is recorded when **Record masked calls** is on. For how your team places and takes calls, see [Make and take calls](/guides/calls/use-the-dialer).
